Those are the ones that read your argument rather than your formatting, and they catch problems the other 12 structurally cannot. AI checks — provider and API key 4 of the 16 checks need one. The other 12 always run.

Any provider with an OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions endpoint works. Your key stays in this browser, is sent to your own local server for each run, and is never saved to disk, written to a log, or kept in your history.
